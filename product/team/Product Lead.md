# Product Lead

## Role
Owns Phase A outcome, scope, priorities, and release sign-off.

## Responsibilities
- Define PRD, user journeys, and acceptance criteria for audio-first localization.
- Prioritize backlog and enforce must/should/could boundaries.
- Resolve cross-team trade-offs (quality vs speed vs cost).
- Run weekly stakeholder reviews and decide go/no-go for release.

## Inputs Needed
- Architecture constraints from System Architect.
- Quality capabilities from ML Lead + QA & Annotation Lead.
- Delivery confidence and risk reports from engineering.

## Outputs Produced
- Phase A scope lock and milestone plan.
- Prioritized backlog and sprint goals.
- Release checklist and final approval decision.

## Immediate Next Steps
1. Publish Phase A PRD and KPI targets.
2. Freeze MVP scope and non-goals.
3. Approve week 1 execution plan and staffing allocation.

## Handover To
**System Architect**

## Handover Package
- Finalized acceptance criteria.
- KPI thresholds and release constraints.
- Priority order of technical capabilities.
